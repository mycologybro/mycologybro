Lion's Mane

Hericium erinaceus

A distinctive edible mushroom with growing scientific interest in cognitive function, the nervous system, mood and general wellbeing.

Discover more
Potential benefits

Traditional mushroom. Modern research.

Lion's Mane has attracted increasing scientific interest. The evidence is promising in several areas, although some potential benefits remain under investigation.

🧠 Cognitive Support

Research suggests Lion's Mane may support memory, concentration and aspects of cognitive function. Early human studies have reported improvements in some measures of memory and attention.

🧬 Nervous System Support

Lion's Mane contains hericenones and erinacines that have been studied for their ability to stimulate nerve growth factor (NGF), creating interest in its potential role in nervous-system health.

😌 Mood, Stress & Anxiety

Small human studies have found promising results for mood, anxiety and feelings of stress. Further research is needed to establish the extent of these effects.

🌿 Menopause & Women's Wellbeing

A small four-week human study involving 30 women investigated Lion's Mane in relation to menopausal wellbeing and reported improvements in measures of depression and general menopausal complaints. More research is needed.

🛡️ Antioxidant Support

Lion's Mane contains compounds with antioxidant activity that may help protect cells from oxidative stress.

🔥 Healthy Inflammatory Response

Laboratory and animal research suggests Lion's Mane may have anti-inflammatory properties which could contribute to general wellbeing.

🦠 Immune Support

Research has identified immune-modulating effects from compounds found in Lion's Mane, although the significance of these effects in humans is still being investigated.

🌱 Gut & Digestive Health

Early research suggests Lion's Mane may influence the gut microbiome and digestive health. More human research is needed.

Safety information

Warnings & contraindications

If you take regular medication or have a medical condition, speak with a doctor or pharmacist before using this product.

Allergy

Do not use if you have a known allergy or sensitivity to Lion's Mane or other mushrooms.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

The safety of Lion's Mane during pregnancy and breastfeeding has not been adequately established. Consult a healthcare professional before use.

Blood-thinning & antiplatelet medication

Lion's Mane has demonstrated antiplatelet effects in laboratory research. This may potentially increase the risk of bruising or bleeding when taken with medicines that also affect blood clotting.

Examples include warfarin, apixaban (Eliquis), rivaroxaban (Xarelto), dabigatran (Pradaxa), heparin, clopidogrel (Plavix) and aspirin.

Diabetes medication

Some research suggests Lion's Mane may affect blood glucose levels. When combined with medication that lowers blood sugar, this may potentially contribute to blood glucose becoming too low.

Immunosuppressant medication

Lion's Mane has demonstrated immune-modulating effects in laboratory and animal research. It may potentially interfere with medicines intended to suppress immune activity.

Surgery

Because of the potential effects on platelet aggregation, consult your doctor before using Lion's Mane if you are due to undergo surgery or an invasive medical procedure.

Possible side effects

Lion's Mane is generally well tolerated in the limited human studies available, but side effects can occur.

  • Gastric upset or gastrointestinal discomfort
  • Nausea
  • Abdominal discomfort
  • Diarrhoea or changes in bowel movements
  • Allergic reaction or sensitivity, particularly in people with a mushroom allergy

Important

The potential benefits described on this website are based on a combination of human research, laboratory studies and traditional use. Some effects remain under investigation. Lion's Mane should not be considered a medicine or a treatment for any medical condition.

Food supplement — not a medicine.

If you experience an adverse reaction, discontinue use. If symptoms are severe or persistent, seek medical advice.

Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and keep the bottle securely closed.
