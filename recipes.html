Recipe 01 · Agar

🍯 Honey Agar

  • 5 g light malt extract
  • 12 g agar
  • 12 g honey
  • 500 ml water
Recipe 02 · Agar

🖤 Charcoal Ryzo Agar

  • 10 g agar
  • 6 g LME
  • 0.75 g nutritional yeast
  • 2 g activated charcoal
Recipe 03 · Substrate

🍄 Master's Mix

  • 150 g hardwood fuel pellets
  • 150 g soy hulls
  • 450 g water, added after pellets are broken down

Total: 750 g wet — enough for one jar.

Recipe 04 · Substrate

🥥 Coir / Vermiculite Sub

  • 1 brick coir (650 g)
  • 2 quarts (~2 L) vermiculite
  • ~4 L boiling water
